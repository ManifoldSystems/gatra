# Sub-500M Edge Architecture: validation vs `efficient-llm-architecture-edge-under-500m-2026-10-20`

> [!tip] Terkait
> - [[pretrain_llms/gatra/docs/efficient-llm-architecture-edge-under-500m-2026-10-20|Baseline doc 2026-10-20]]
> - [[pretrain_llms/gatra/README|Gatra]]

Scope: verify §2–§4 + §7 of the baseline against primary sources only (arXiv, official `config.json`/model cards, vendor docs). Fetched 2026-10-22.

## 1. Validated claims

**(a) Depth beats width below 1B.** MobileLLM spec table confirms 30L/576/9Q-3KV @124.6M and 32L/960/15Q-5KV @345.3M ([Table 9](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14905v2)). Quote: *"models with 30 or even 42 layers perform significantly better than those with 12 layers for transformer models sized around 125M"* ([arXiv:2402.14905](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14905)). Embedding-share saves *"16M, approximately 11.8% of total parameters with a 0.2 points drop"* — restored by adding layers (same source). SmolLM2 (30L/576 @135M; 32L/960 @360M, [config 135M](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M/raw/main/config.json), [config 360M](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-360M/raw/main/config.json)) follows the same shape.

**(b) GQA + KV-cache math.** *"uptrained GQA achieves quality close to multi-head attention with comparable speed to MQA"* using *"5% of original pre-training compute"* ([Ainslie et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.298/)). Shipped ratios (from official configs): Qwen2.5-0.5B 14Q/2KV ([config](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B/raw/main/config.json)), SmolLM2 9/3 and 15/5, Qwen3-0.6B 16/8 ([config](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B/raw/main/config.json)), MiniCPM4-0.5B 16/2, LFM2-350M 16/8 ([config](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2-350M/raw/main/config.json)), Falcon-H1-0.5B 8/2 ([config](https://huggingface.co/tiiuae/Falcon-H1-0.5B-Base/raw/main/config.json)). KV/token formula re-verified: Qwen2.5 12 KiB, SmolLM2-135M 22.5 KiB, SmolLM2-360M 40 KiB (bf16). Baseline §2.5 trace (352M, 38.75 KiB/tok, 155 MiB @4K) recomputes exactly.

**(c) RoPE default; ALiBi/NoPE unused in shipped models.** Every released ≤500M model uses RoPE except Granite-4.0-H-350M (`position_embedding_type: nope`, [config](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-4.0-h-350m/raw/main/config.json)). Thetas from configs: SmolLM2 both 100k, Qwen2.5 1e6, Qwen3 1e6, LFM2 1e6, Falcon-H1 1e11. NoPE paper: *"NoPE outperforms other explicit positional encoding methods while requiring no additional computation"* — but only on length-generalization reasoning tasks ([arXiv:2305.19466](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19466)). No shipped sub-500M model uses NoPE-as-position (Granite-H's nope rides on Mamba layers), so baseline's "RoPE default" stands.

**(d) SwiGLU ≈2.67×.** *"for the GLU-variant-based FFN layers, which have three weight matrices instead of two, we reduce the hidden layer to d_ff=2048"* (from 3072 @d=768) ([arXiv:2002.05202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05202)). Exact in SmolLM2 (1536/576, 2560/960) and Granite-H (2048/768). See §2 for the two shipped counter-examples.

**(e) Norms/stability.** *"RMSNorm is computationally simpler and thus more efficient than LayerNorm"* ([arXiv:1910.07467](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.07467)). QK-norm ships in LFM2 conv/GQA blocks, Gemma-3-270M (`use_qk_norm=True`, [source](https://github.com/google-deepmind/gemma/blob/main/gemma/gm/nn/_gemma.py)), Qwen3 (*"introduce QK-Norm to ensure stable training"*, [report](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09388v1)), Falcon-H1 (attention multipliers in config). Gemma 2 soft-cap: *"50.0 for self-attention, 30.0 for the final layer"* ([report](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00118)). No controlled ≤500M ablation exists for any of these — baseline's "cheap insurance" framing is correct.

**(f) Vocab/tying economics.** *"we recommend tying the input embedding and this output embedding"* ([arXiv:1608.05859](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05859)). Qwen2.5-0.5B: 0.49B total / 0.36B non-embedding → 136M (28%) in embeddings ([model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B)). Gemma 3 270M: *"170 million embedding parameters… and 100 million for transformer blocks"* with 262k vocab ([Google blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/introducing-gemma-3-270m/)). Tying confirmed in SmolLM2, Qwen2.5/3, Granite-H, LFM2.5 configs.

**(g) Hybrid: real models, runtime-gated.** LFM2-350M: 16 blocks (10 gated short-conv + 6 GQA), 10T tokens, distilled full-run from LFM1-7B, vendor-claimed *"2x faster decode and prefill than Qwen3 on CPU"* ([Liquid blog](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/liquid-foundation-models-v2-our-second-series-of-generative-ai-models)). Granite-H-350M: 4 attention + 28 Mamba-2, NoPE, ~14.5T tokens (10T+2T+2T+0.5T stages, [base card](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-4.0-h-350m)). Falcon-H1-0.5B: parallel hybrid-head, MMLU 55.0 / GSM8K 60.2 / HumanEval 36.0, but 521M total and Falcon license ([model card](https://huggingface.co/tiiuae/Falcon-H1-0.5B-Base)). Mamba-2 370M: *"near-perfect retrieval on a 256K context length, outperforming similarly sized transformer models"* after continued pretraining ([arXiv:2410.07145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07145)). Runtime maturity improved since baseline: llama.cpp merged Falcon-H1 state-256 `SSM_SCAN` CUDA support ([PR #14602](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/14602)) and Granite-4.0 hybrid support ([PR #13550](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/13550)); ExecuTorch ships LFM2/LFM2.5 8da4w XNNPACK export configs; LFM2.5-350M claims day-one llama.cpp/MLX/vLLM, 313 tok/s (AMD CPU) / 188 tok/s (Snapdragon Gen4), 28T tokens ([LFM2.5 blog](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-5-350m-no-size-left-behind)).

**(h) No ternary evidence below 700M.** BitNet b1.58 @700M: PPL 12.87 vs 12.33 FP-LLaMA; parity only at 3B (9.91 vs 10.04) ([arXiv:2402.17764](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17764)). BitNet-2B4T (2B, 4T tokens) is the smallest native-1-bit release — still 3× above 700M. MatMul-free 370M (24L, 15B tok): avg 40.3 vs Transformer++ 41.1 ([arXiv:2406.02528](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02528)). Baseline's "bf16 + QAT int4" conclusion stands.

**(i) MoE / MTP / early-exit don't fit.** No controlled ≤500M study found for any of the three. Adjacent only: LFM2's MoE variant is 8.3B-total/1.5B-active (above scale); MTP appears solely as co-trained speculative-draft heads (e.g. Gemma-class EAGLE-style drafters), never as a ≤500M main-model objective; early-exit appears only in edge-cloud speculative setups (Vicuna-68M draft + 7B target). Baseline's "skip" stands, with the drafter nuance added.

## 2. Corrected / updated claims (baseline → primary source)

1. **SmolLM2-135M θ is 100k, not 10k.** Baseline §3.3 says θ=10k (2K/8K). Both configs read `"rope_theta": 100000`. The §7.1 recommendation (θ=10k @2K ctx) is unaffected, but its cited evidence was wrong.
2. **LFM2-350M FFN is 6656, not 4608.** Baseline §4 table says 4608; config reads `"block_ff_dim": 6656` (same as LFM2.5-350M, which the baseline lists correctly). With d=1024 that is a 6.5× FFN ratio — far above the 2.67× recommendation, funded by having only 6 attention layers.
3. **Qwen3-0.6B is ~596M params, not 751.6M.** Baseline §4 table says 751.6M; the [model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B) states 0.6B total / 0.44B non-embedding (recomputes: 155.6M embed + 28×~15.7M layers ≈ 596M). Still above the 500M line, so reference-only status is unchanged.
4. **Falcon-H1-0.5B does NOT tie embeddings** (`"tie_word_embeddings": false`). At 32,784 vocab × 1024 this costs ~33.6M params (~6.4% of the model) — a quiet tax the baseline doesn't mention.
5. **MobileLLM-R1 breaks the depth rule — and still wins at reasoning.** R1 uses only 15 layers + 128k vocab + 4.2T tokens + KD from Llama-3.1-8B (mid-training), reaching 360M: GSM8K 39.4 / MATH500 13.4 / HumanEval 32.9 ([GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/MobileLLM/blob/main/projects/MobileLLM-R1/README.md)). Depth-30 guidance holds for from-scratch base models; the reasoning-distillation regime (wide + big-vocab + teacher) is an unresolved exception, not a refutation.
6. **Two shipped models use 4× FFN, not 2.67×.** MiniCPM4-0.5B: 4096/1024 with LongRoPE ([config](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM4-0.5B-mlx/commit/469201a6837b45b1c008ecd05743e951c3a95d0f)) and MobileLLM-R1-360M: 4096/1024. The 2.67× rule is the best-supported default, but 4× has production existence proof — treat as open, not settled.
7. **Qwen2.5-0.5B has QKV bias; Qwen3 removes it and adds QK-norm** ([Qwen2.5 card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B); [Qwen3 report](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09388v1)). Baseline's blanket `attention_bias=false` tier spec sides with Qwen3 — fine, but the lineage fact was missing.
8. **Gemma-3-270M attention is 5:1 local:global, SW=512, head_dim=256, 4Q/1KV** with local θ=10k / global θ=1e6 ([source](https://github.com/google-deepmind/gemma/blob/main/gemma/gm/nn/_gemma.py)). Baseline's "θ scales with context" is underspecified: Gemma scales θ *per layer type*, and its KV math is dominated by the 512-token local window, not full 32K.
9. **Runtime caution softened for specific pairs.** Baseline §3.7 says hybrid needs mature kernels "or theoretical gains evaporate." Now confirmed: llama.cpp covers Falcon-H1 + Granite-4.0-H; ExecuTorch covers LFM2/LFM2.5 (XNNPACK 8da4w); MLX covers LFM2.5 day-one. The rule stands for *unlisted* model×runtime pairs.

## 3. Open questions

- **No distillation scaling law below 546M students** (Buschütz fits ≥546M). MobileLLM (KD loses, 2.6–3.2× cost) vs LFM2 (full-run KD from LFM1-7B wins) vs Gemma 2 (KD wins) remain irreconcilable without a ≤500M A/B.
- **Hymba small weights unreleased** (only 1.5B on HF); paper's token counts (1T/250B/50B for 125M/350M/1.5B) look inverted vs size — flag as reported-but-suspicious ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13676)).
- **No first-party phone tok/s for SmolLM2/Qwen2.5**; LFM2.5's 313/188 tok/s and Gemma-3-270M's *"0.75% battery for 25 conversations"* are vendor-internal, unverified.
- **No controlled ≤500M ablations** for ORPO/GRPO, fp8, QAT-vs-PTQ, sliding-window+sink policy (StreamingLLM validated on ≥2.9B only: *"just 4 initial tokens sufficing"* ([arXiv:2309.17453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17453))), or θ selection (both SmolLM2 sizes share θ=100k — looks copy-pasted, not ablated).
- **Qwen2.5-0.5B "18T" and Qwen3 "36T" are family-corpus totals**, per-size allocations undisclosed ([report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15115)) — token/param ratios in baseline §5.1 for Qwen rows are upper bounds, not measurements.
